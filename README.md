# IBM Employee Attrition Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition using the IBM HR Analytics dataset. The main objective is to identify factors that contribute to employee turnover and provide insights that can help organizations improve retention.

## Objectives
- Analyze employee attrition patterns
- Identify important factors affecting attrition
- Generate business insights and recommendations for retention

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset
The project uses the IBM Employee Attrition dataset, which includes employee information such as age, department, job role, salary, overtime, years at company, and attrition status.

## Analysis Performed
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Exploratory data analysis
- Correlation analysis
- Attrition analysis by job role
- Attrition analysis by income level
- Attrition analysis by years at company
- Attrition analysis by overtime status
- Data visualization to highlight key findings

## Key Insights
- Employees who work overtime show higher attrition
- Lower income employees tend to leave more frequently
- Some job roles experience higher attrition than others
- Employees with fewer years at the company are more likely to leave

## Business Recommendations
- Review workload and reduce excessive overtime
- Improve retention efforts for employees in high-risk job roles
- Consider compensation strategies for employees with lower income levels
- Strengthen onboarding and early employee engagement programs
